( Mission )

We help Asian designer brands land in New York — fashion's center — with their sensibility intact and none of the operational weight.

01 — Who we are

A curator and a bridge.

Raw Thoughts curates original designer brands from Seoul and across Asia, and helps them grow on the global stage. Through a high-sensibility online store and regular pop-ups in New York's fashion district, we give U.S. audiences the fastest, most vivid way to discover new Asian design.

02 — The flagship

Ephemera is how Raw Thoughts shows up in New York. A curated online store and a recurring pop-up in the fashion district — a fast, living touchpoint between Asian brands and American shoppers.
